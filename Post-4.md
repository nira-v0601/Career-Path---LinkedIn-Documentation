## Context
GTU university exams arrived.
Plan was to continue Career Path preparation alongside
exam preparation — reality turned out harder than expected.

---

## Honest Reflection

**What Happened:**
- Maintaining both exam preparation and career preparation simultaneously
  proved difficult
- Mind suggested delaying career preparation until exams conclude
- Root cause identified: procrastination throughout the semester
  led to heavy last-minute exam load

**Key Insight:**
Procrastination doesn't just affect the present moment —
it creates future scenarios where everything piles up at once,
making even small additional tasks feel like a burden.

---

## The Clarity Found

After thinking through the full daily schedule:

> *"Instead of doing random things for relaxing,
> why not continue the Career Path?
> Even 15–20 minutes is enough to learn something new —
> and it also boosts mood and motivation to continue
> the rest of exam preparation."*

**The shift:** Random relaxation → Purposeful micro-learning

---

## Plan Going Forward
- Apply 15–20 min daily Career Path sessions during exam period
- Update progress and effect in the upcoming post
